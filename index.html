About the Workshop

This workshop focuses on emerging Artificial Intelligence (AI) applications across the private markets ecosystem, including private equity, private credit, venture capital, real assets and other alternative investments.

The workshop will bring together researchers, practitioners, asset managers, allocators, data providers, and technology experts to discuss how machine learning, large language models, agentic AI, and emerging technologies can transform investment decision-making — including bringing speed, transparency and better accountability around sourcing, diligence, valuations, liquidity and risk management and operational decision-making in private markets.

Private markets are a growing area for AI in finance, but limited disclosure, inconsistent reporting, sparse transactions, and stale valuations make them difficult to analyze. Unlike many financial machine learning settings, private markets have incomplete data, scarce labels, and outcomes that may take years to observe. AI systems must combine varied evidence, track sources, quantify uncertainty, and support human judgment.

Call for Papers

We invite submissions on any topics at the intersection of AI and private markets (all asset classes, broadly defined), topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • AI for investment analysis, deal sourcing, due diligence, and valuation
  • Agentic and multi-agent systems for the investment lifecycle
  • Foundation models for financial applications
  • NLP, knowledge extraction, and information retrieval for private markets
  • Portfolio construction, similarity analysis, and benchmarking
  • Trustworthy, interpretable, and responsible AI in finance
  • AI as an investment theme and risk factor
  • Risk management, monitoring, and reporting
